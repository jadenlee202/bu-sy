## Notes

- On a day whose UTC offset changes, the source can label both passes of a repeated hour with the same offset, so instants there may be off by an hour. Those days are listed above and left out of playback and typical traffic; their rows stay in the clean file, sorted by the stated instant.
- Intervals under 5 minutes are short bursts of readings (e.g. every 30 seconds), not duplicates; time weighting gives each reading only the time until the next one.
- Counts are kept as recorded. Zeros (often overnight) are real readings; missing time is never filled.

## Gap policy

A count holds until the next reading, for at most 30 minutes, and never past local midnight. Time beyond that is *no data*, never zero. Days with an offset change are left out of playback and typical traffic because a wall-clock hour repeats or is skipped.
