## What these numbers are

Purchases, not occupancy. A transaction is one checkout: it can cover several menus or a weighed buffet plate, one person can pay for others, and nobody's arrival, departure or seat is recorded. Any translation into people present is a model (see `data/generate_campus_simulation.py`).

## Transformations

- Read the semicolon-separated file; rejected rows with a missing ID or canteen, an unrecognised trans_date, a date that disagrees with trans_date, or unparseable numbers (listed above).
- Grouped rows into transactions by (canteen, date, transaction_id); each transaction counts once, however many article lines or menus it has. Lines identical to another line of the same transaction are kept inside that one transaction (they look like the same article bought twice).
- Dropped transactions with two different clock times; left out refunds/cancellations (all lines negative).
- Read trans_date as local wall-clock time (raw text unchanged in the original file).
- Counted purchases per canteen in 15-minute intervals from each canteen-day's first to last sale; empty intervals inside that span are zeros.
