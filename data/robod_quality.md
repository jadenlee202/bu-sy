## Transformations

- Parsed `timestamp` with its +08:00 offset; no timezone conversion.
- Used `occupant_count [number]` only. Rejected rows with an unparseable timestamp, or a missing, non-numeric, fractional or negative count (listed above). Zero is a reading, kept as zero.
- Collapsed identical duplicate instants; dropped conflicting ones.
- Sorted by instant (stable); kept only facility_id, timestamp, occupancy_count.
