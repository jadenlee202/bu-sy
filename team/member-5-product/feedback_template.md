## Why three separate columns

To keep the research honest, each observation is split into:

1. **What the participant actually said / did** — verbatim or close paraphrase,
   no spin.
2. **Team interpretation** — our reading of what it means (clearly ours, not
   theirs).
3. **Untested assumption** — anything we're inferring that we have **not**
   verified and would need to test.

Never let column 2 or 3 leak into column 1.
