# BU-sy actionable plans: five-task usability protocol

Build: local feature/actionable-plans. Protocol only: no participants, feedback
or results have been collected. Allow 10-15 minutes per student.

Begin on the planner with reset preferences. Use the selected demo time, not
the device clock. Ask students to think aloud. Give one task at a time without
explaining controls first. Record help given; use anonymous participant codes.

| Task | Read aloud | Facilitator notes |
|---|---|---|
| Study before class | You are at Mugar at 3 PM, with an hour until class at Warren. Find somewhere to study, choose a plan and tell me when to leave. | Study minimum 30. Let the student check access. Ask what preparation and cleanup mean. |
| Tight lunch | You are at West at noon with 30 minutes before a commitment at Warren. Find lunch, or explain why it cannot fit. Show a change you would consider. | Do not enlarge the original window for them. Observe whether suggested changes are understood as changed inputs. |
| Workout including changing | At 3 PM you are at Mugar with 80 minutes before class at Warren. Plan at least 35 minutes of exercise including changing and packing up. | Let them edit the 5 + 5 allowances and check membership, waiver and card requirements. FitRec is one building. |
| Unknown starting point | You have an hour to study but have not decided where you will start. Browse options. Can you follow one as a confirmed travel plan yet? | Leave start unset; observe travel-not-checked wording, then ask them to complete travel details. |
| Change a selected plan | Choose a study plan from Mugar with an hour free before Warren. Your next destination changes to Fenway. Update the plan and tell me which timeline you would follow. | Observe Edit inputs, preserved inputs, immediate invalidation and recalculation. The visit may stop fitting; do not prompt a fabricated success. |

For every task, complete this blank record:

- Participant code / task / viewport / theme / build:
- Time to first useful option (seconds; if none, record that and time to explanation):
- Completion: independent / with help / not completed:
- Understood travel allowances and departure time? Evidence or exact words:
- Noticed that busyness is simulated? Evidence or exact words:
- Any access or preparation/cleanup detail making the plan unsuitable:
- Would follow the suggestion? Why or why not:
- Assistance, errors and other observations:

After the tasks ask what information they need before following a real plan.
Keep findings separate from this protocol; do not turn missing answers into
positive feedback.
